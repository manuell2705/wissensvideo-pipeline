About Mindsphere

Mindsphere is an English-language YouTube channel that explains science topics – the brain, space, nature and technology – in clear, short videos.

Watch us on YouTube: youtube.com/@MindsphereExplains

About the Mindsphere Pipeline app

“Mindsphere Pipeline” is a private tool used only by the Mindsphere team. It helps us produce our videos and upload them to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and it does not access anyone else’s account.

Details on how data is handled are in our Privacy Policy.
