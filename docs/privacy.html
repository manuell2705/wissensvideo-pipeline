Who we are

This policy covers the Mindsphere YouTube channel and the “Mindsphere Pipeline” app, an internal tool the Mindsphere team uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to use the YouTube Data API on behalf of that one Mindsphere channel. It uses this access only to:

  • upload videos made by Mindsphere to the Mindsphere channel,
  • set the title, description, tags, thumbnail and privacy status of those videos,
  • read basic information about the Mindsphere channel and its videos.

The app does not access any other person’s account, and it does not collect data from viewers of our videos.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored encrypted in our automation tool (n8n Cloud) and is used only to perform the actions listed above. We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

Mindsphere Pipeline’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google account security page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the app can no longer upload or read anything.

Viewers of our videos

When you watch Mindsphere videos, YouTube processes your data under its own policies. We only see the anonymous statistics YouTube provides to every channel.

Contact

Questions? Contact us via our YouTube channel: youtube.com/@MindsphereExplains.
